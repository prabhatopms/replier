Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 26, 2025

Short version: InstaReplier stores all your data locally on your device. We operate no servers, collect no analytics, and never see your messages, contacts, or API keys.

1. What We Collect

InstaReplier stores the following data exclusively on your device using Chrome's local IndexedDB storage:

  • Instagram contact profiles you interact with (display name, relationship notes, tone preferences)
  • DM message history scraped from Instagram for the purpose of generating contextual replies
  • AI-generated conversation summaries and structured memory (topics, communication style, etc.)
  • Your AI provider API keys (Claude / Gemini / OpenAI), stored in Chrome's local extension storage
  • Extension settings and preferences (selected tone, conversation goals, auto-pilot state)

2. How We Use Your Data

Data stored locally is used solely to:

  • Build conversation context for generating personalized AI replies
  • Remember your tone, goal, and relationship settings per contact
  • Avoid generating duplicate or previously seen replies

When you trigger a reply generation, your conversation history and settings are sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected (Anthropic, Google, or OpenAI). This request is made using your own API key. InstaReplier never proxies, intercepts, or logs this traffic.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share your data with any third parties, full stop. The only outbound network calls made by InstaReplier are:

  • api.anthropic.com — if you use Claude (Anthropic's API)
  • generativelanguage.googleapis.com — if you use Gemini (Google's API)
  • api.openai.com — if you use GPT-4 / OpenAI's API

These calls are governed by each provider's own privacy policy. InstaReplier has no servers and no analytics infrastructure.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

All data lives in your browser and is under your full control:

  • Delete a contact from the side panel to remove all their data, messages, and summaries
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data
  • Clearing Chrome's site data for the extension also wipes the database

We have no ability to delete data on your behalf because we never have access to it.

5. Permissions Explained

  • storage — saves your settings and contacts in Chrome local storage
  • sidePanel — displays the InstaReplier panel in Chrome's built-in side panel
  • activeTab / tabs — reads the active Instagram tab to detect messages and render the reply popover
  • Host permissions (instagram.com, etc.) — required for the content script to read the conversation DOM and inject the UI
  • Host permissions (AI provider APIs) — required to call the AI API directly from the browser without a proxy

6. Children's Privacy

InstaReplier is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the "Last updated" date at the top will change. Significant changes will be noted in the extension's changelog on the Chrome Web Store.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Open an issue on the project's GitHub repository or email the developer directly.

InstaReplier is an independent open-source project and is not affiliated with Meta, Instagram, Anthropic, Google, or OpenAI.
